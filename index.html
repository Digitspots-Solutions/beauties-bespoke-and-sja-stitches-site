About Us

Bepmop Cakes Limited is a leading cake manufacturer and bakery based in Lagos, Nigeria. We specialize in producing handcrafted celebration cakes, dessert platters, and sweet confections using premium ingredients and creative design. Our mission is to bring joy to every celebration with beautiful cakes that taste as good as they look. Operating from 11, Oyedele Ogunniyi Street in Anthony, Lagos, we serve birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and special occasions across Kosofe and beyond.
