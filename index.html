About Us

At Beauties Bespoke and SJA Stitches, we are passionate about creating fashion that tells your story. Located in the heart of Kosofe, Lagos, our studio combines traditional tailoring expertise with contemporary design innovation. Every stitch, every seam, every detail is executed with meticulous care to ensure your garments fit perfectly and reflect your personal style.

Whether you're preparing for a special occasion or building your everyday wardrobe, we bring decades of experience and an unwavering commitment to quality. Our team takes pride in understanding your needs, offering expert guidance, and delivering pieces that make you look and feel extraordinary.

Our Services

Bespoke Tailoring

Custom-fitted garments designed specifically for your body and style preferences. From traditional attire to modern fashion, we create pieces that fit like a dream.

Creative Design Consultation

Work directly with our designers to bring your fashion vision to life. We guide you through fabric selection, style choices, and design elements.

Wedding & Special Occasions

Corporate & Casual Wear
