About Us

Bepmop Cakes Poultry Tech Limited (also known as Bepmop Cakes Livestock Foods Nigeria) is a leading FoodTech company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We specialize in producing premium ready-to-eat foods and high-quality livestock feeds that meet international standards. Our mission is to provide busy Nigerians with nutritious, delicious meals while supporting the agricultural sector with innovative feed solutions. Operating from our facility at 11, Oyedele Ogunniyi Street in Anthony, Lagos, we combine traditional Nigerian flavors with modern food technology to serve homes, offices, and farms across Kosofe and beyond. Every product from Bepmop is crafted with care, hygiene, and a commitment to excellence.

Enjoy our delicious selection of freshly prepared Nigerian meals delivered straight to your doorstep.
